C1 · executive summary

Routine OEB5 operation2027-10
Upgrade objectiveInstallation and qualification (+3 months)

Routine OEB5 operation of the existing C1 module

• Scope: fixed isolators for material dispensing on the upper floor and final packing on the ground floor; HVAC upgrade; interlocked inlet and outlet airlocks with a mist shower; custom flexible isolators covering each unit operation and the waste streams.

• Estimate: budget quotations are in hand. ILC Dover, flexible isolator for reactor charging, £115,800 for the package, including concept design of the R19–R22 charging arrangement. Howorth, single-chamber dispensing isolator, £250,000 each. Both are ex works and exclude packing, transport, installation and commissioning.

• Selection: this is an upgrade of the existing facility, with local alterations rather than installation of a complete new machine, so a China-sourced isolator is not recommended. Every part placed in the UK has to meet UK standards and carry the CE mark; retrospective certification adds at least 3–4 months, and the extra cost and time are not a good trade.

• Linked delivery: it has to finish with the in-building retrofit (preparative HPLC and lyophiliser) in order to give preparative HPLC an OEB5 operating capability.

• Programme: assumed to be approved together with the retrofit. The preparative HPLC unit drives the C1 delivery timeline.

14 / 31

C1 · isolator selection

CE mark requiredOff-the-shelf high integrity
Condition of UK placementThe type this project needs

A China-sourced isolator is not recommended

• Anything placed in the UK has to meet UK standards and carry the CE mark. The China-sourced option does not currently carry CE.

• Retrospective CE marking adds at least 3–4 months on the experience of other projects, and longer if certification requires design changes.

• The design and test documentation that CE marking requires was not prepared against that standard, so assembling it is a substantial piece of work.

• This project needs an off-the-shelf high-integrity isolator. Information received from the China suppliers approached has not been usable as it stands. Tofflon mainly builds isolators bespoke to a piece of equipment, which is not this requirement.

• Recommended route: develop the supply on the Howorth and ILC Dover budget quotations already in hand.
